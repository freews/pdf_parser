8.1.19 Power Measurement and Reporting

Power measurement and reporting is an optional set of independent capabilities which allows the host to utilize interval power measurements from one or more embedded power sensors in the NVM subsystem.

An interval power measurement is the smallest time-based power measurement reportable and used by the NVM subsystem. Each interval power measurement of a power measurement type shall:

• be the average, over a one second interval, of the power measurement samples that are taken with a sampling rate of less than or equal to the value reported in the Interval Power Measurement Sample Rate (IPMSR) field in the Identify Controller data structure (refer to Figure 328);
• be taken over non-overlapping one second intervals; and
• be based on measured power, unless otherwise specified.

Figure 679 defines the power measurement types for power measurement and reporting capabilities and fields. The power measurement type specifies what power source to measure. A power measurement type of NVM subsystem total power is the aggregate power consumed by all components within the NVM subsystem.
