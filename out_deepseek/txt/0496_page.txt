### Figure 531: Discovery Information Management – Data

| Bytes | O/M\(^1\) | Description |
|-------|-----------|-------------|
| ...   | O         |             |
| TDL - 1: TDL - TEL | \(M^3\) | Discovery Information Entry NUMENT-1: This field contains the last discovery information entry as defined in Figure 532 for extended discovery information entries (if present) and as defined in Figure 310 for basic discovery information entries (if present), where TEL is the size specified in the Total Entry Length (TEL) field of the extended discovery information entry and TDL is the size specified in the Total Data Length (TDL) field. For basic discovery information entries, TEL shall be 1,024 bytes. |

**Notes:**
1. O/M definition: O = Optional, M = Mandatory.
2. This field is optional for a register, de-register, or update task performed by a DDC. For a register, de-register, or update task performed by a host or CDC, this field shall be cleared to 0h.
3. This field is mandatory for an update task. For a register or de-register task, this field is optional.
