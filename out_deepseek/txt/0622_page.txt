• while the CSTS.SHST field is set to 10b (i.e., shutdown processing complete) in all controllers in the NVM subsystem.

A set of interval power measurements may be contiguous or non-contiguous in time. A set of interval power measurements is contiguous if they are from a continuous period of time without any gaps. Examples of actions that are able to cause a set of interval power measurements to be non-contiguous include:

• removing power from the NVM subsystem (i.e., a power cycle);
• entering a non-operational power state;
• initiating an NVM Subsystem Reset; and
• the CSTS.SHST field becoming set to 10b (i.e., shutdown processing complete) in all controllers in the NVM subsystem.

If the cumulative operational energy consumed is reported in the Operational Lifetime Energy Consumed (OLEC) field in the SMART / Health Information log page, then interval power measurements shall be collected for that field.

The collection of interval power measurements for the Power Threshold feature and Power Measurement capability are dependent on whether the respective capabilities are enabled.

If:

• an interval power measurement is reported in the Interval Power Measurement (IPM) field in the SMART / Health Information log page;
• the cumulative operational energy consumed is reported in the Operational Lifetime Energy Consumed (OLEC) field in the SMART / Health Information log page;
• the Power Threshold feature is supported; or
• the Power Measurement capability is supported,

then the controller shall set the Interval Power Measurement Sample Rate (IPMSR) field in the Identify Controller data structure (refer to Figure 328) to a non-zero value.
