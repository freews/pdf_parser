#### 8.3.2.3.4.3 {(NQN, IP, Protocol), Role} Zone Member Type (Type 2h)  

<|ref|>text<|/ref|><|det|>[[114, 113, 883, 158]]<|/det|>
This Zone member type identifies a specific fabric interface (i.e., through the IP address) and the specific IP protocol (e.g., TCP) used by the NVMe- oF entity identified by the Zone member's NQN over that fabric interface. The format of this Zone member type is shown in Figure 744.
