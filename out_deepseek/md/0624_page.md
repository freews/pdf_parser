<|ref|>text<|/ref|><|det|>[[143, 90, 883, 165]]<|/det|>
- the Power Measurement Count (PMC) field in the Power Measurement log page is set to a non-zero value; and- at least one interval power measurement logged in the Power Measurement log page is greater than the maximum power indicated by the last Power Histogram Descriptor (i.e., Power Histogram Descriptor NPDH-1, refer to Figure 297),  

<|ref|>text<|/ref|><|det|>[[116, 170, 883, 203]]<|/det|>
then the controller shall set Power Histogram Descriptor Overflow (PHD0) bit to '1' in the Power Measurement log page.  

<|ref|>text<|/ref|><|det|>[[115, 207, 883, 253]]<|/det|>
If the controller is collecting interval power measurements for the Power Measurement log page (i.e., the PME bit is set to '1'), then the controller shall use each interval power measurement (i.e., average of power measurement samples over one second, refer to section 8.1.19) to:  

<|ref|>text<|/ref|><|det|>[[143, 258, 883, 300]]<|/det|>
a) update the Average Interval Power field (i.e., calculate the average of the interval power measurements collected for the Power Measurement log page (refer to Figure 297)); 
b) update the:  

<|ref|>text<|/ref|><|det|>[[173, 308, 753, 340]]<|/det|>
\(\bigcirc\) Maximum Interval Power field (refer to Figure 297); and \(\bigcirc\) Maximum Interval Power Timestamp field (refer to Figure 297), if supported,  

<|ref|>text<|/ref|><|det|>[[171, 346, 883, 376]]<|/det|>
if the interval power measurement is greater than the power indicated in the Maximum Interval Power field;  

<|ref|>text<|/ref|><|det|>[[143, 375, 883, 407]]<|/det|>
c) increment the Power Histogram Bin Count field of the associated Power Histogram Descriptor in the Power Measurement log page (refer to Figure 297), if the interval power measurement is:  

<|ref|>text<|/ref|><|det|>[[173, 411, 883, 457]]<|/det|>
\(\bigcirc\) greater than or equal to the power indicated in the Power Histogram Bin Lower Threshold field of that Power Histogram Descriptor; and \(\bigcirc\) less than the sum of:  

<|ref|>text<|/ref|><|det|>[[260, 463, 883, 509]]<|/det|>
- the power indicated in the Power Histogram Bin Lower Threshold field of that Power Histogram Descriptor; and- the power indicated in the Power Histogram Bin Size field (refer to Figure 297);  

<|ref|>text<|/ref|><|det|>[[143, 513, 883, 544]]<|/det|>
d) increment the Power Measurement Count (PMC) field in the Power Measurement log page (refer to Figure 297); and  

<|ref|>text<|/ref|><|det|>[[143, 543, 883, 604]]<|/det|>
e) increment the Power Histogram Descriptor Overflow Count (PHDOC) field, if the interval power measurement is greater than the maximum power indicated by the last Power Histogram Descriptor (i.e., Power Histogram Descriptor NPDH-1, refer to Figure 297) in the Power Measurement log page.
