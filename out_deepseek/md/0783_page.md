## Annex C. Power Management and Consumption (Informative)  

<|ref|>text<|/ref|><|det|>[[115, 117, 883, 148]]<|/det|>
NVM Express power management capabilities allow the host to manage power for a controller. Power management includes both control and reporting mechanisms.  

<|ref|>text<|/ref|><|det|>[[115, 153, 883, 185]]<|/det|>
For information on transport power management (e.g., PCle, RDMA), refer to the applicable NVM Express Transport specification.  

<|ref|>text<|/ref|><|det|>[[115, 190, 785, 207]]<|/det|>
The scope of NVM Express power management is the controller (refer to section 5.2.26.1.2).  

<|ref|>text<|/ref|><|det|>[[115, 212, 596, 228]]<|/det|>
NVM Express power management uses the following functionality:  

<|ref|>text<|/ref|><|det|>[[144, 234, 246, 249]]<|/det|>
a) Features:  

<|ref|>text<|/ref|><|det|>[[172, 256, 884, 336]]<|/det|>
Power Management (refer to section 5.2.26.1.2 and section 8.1.18); Autonomous Power State Transition (refer to section 5.2.26.1.6 and section 8.1.18.2); Non- Operational Power State Configuration (refer to section 5.2.26.1.10 and section 8.1.18.1); and Spinup Control (refer to section 5.2.26.1.18);  

<|ref|>text<|/ref|><|det|>[[143, 340, 541, 355]]<|/det|>
b) NVM subsystem workloads (refer to 8.1.18.3); and  

<|ref|>text<|/ref|><|det|>[[143, 355, 536, 370]]<|/det|>
c) Runtime D3 transitions (refer to section 8.1.18.4).  

<|ref|>text<|/ref|><|det|>[[115, 376, 883, 407]]<|/det|>
Controller thermal management may cause a transition to a lower power state, interacting with these Features:  

<|ref|>text<|/ref|><|det|>[[143, 412, 884, 473]]<|/det|>
a) Temperature Threshold (refer to section 5.2.26.1.3); 
b) Host Controlled Thermal Management (refer to section 5.2.26.1.9 and section 8.1.18.5); and 
c) access to host memory buffer (refer to section 5.2.26.2.4) may be prohibited in non-operational power state.
