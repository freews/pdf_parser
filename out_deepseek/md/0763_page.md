#### 8.3.5.6.2 AVE Connections  

<|ref|>text<|/ref|><|det|>[[114, 113, 883, 158]]<|/det|>
An NVMe entity (i.e., a host or a controller) connection with a DH- HMAC- CHAP AVE shall use TLS version 1.3 (refer to RFC 8446) with pre- shared key (PSK) authentication, as specified for NVMe/TCP (refer to the NVMe over TCP Transport Specification).  

<|ref|>text<|/ref|><|det|>[[114, 164, 883, 239]]<|/det|>
In order to establish a TLS connection with an AVE, an NVMe entity requires a PSK shared between that entity and the AVE (i.e., \(\mathsf{PSK}_{\mathsf{ea}}\) ) for authentication of the TLS connection. \(\mathsf{PSK}_{\mathsf{ea}}\) shall be either derived from the DH- HMAC- CHAP secret, if the DH- HMAC- CHAP secret representation specifies a hash function (refer to section 8.3.5.5.8), or provisioned on the NVMe entity through a configured PSK, as specified for NVMe/TCP.  

<|ref|>text<|/ref|><|det|>[[114, 244, 883, 276]]<|/det|>
Derivation of \(\mathsf{PSK}_{\mathsf{ea}}\) from a DH- HMAC- CHAP secret shall use the HKDF- Extract and HKDF- Expand- Label operations (refer to RFC 5869 and RFC 8446) in the following order:  

<|ref|>text<|/ref|><|det|>[[128, 281, 883, 327]]<|/det|>
1. PRK = HKDF-Extract(0, DH-HMAC-CHAP secret); and 
2. \(\mathsf{PSK}_{\mathsf{ea}} = \mathsf{HKDEF - Expand - Label}(\mathsf{PRK},\) "A-V-Entity" \(\parallel\) \(\mathsf{NQNAVE}\) \(\mathsf{NQN}_{\mathsf{e}}\) Length(DH-HMAC-CHAP secret)),  

<|ref|>text<|/ref|><|det|>[[114, 332, 883, 377]]<|/det|>
where \(\mathsf{NQNAVE}\) is the NQN of the AVE and \(\mathsf{NQN}_{\mathsf{e}}\) is the NQN of the NVMe entity. The hash function used with HKDF shall be the one specified in the DH- HMAC- CHAP secret representation (refer to section 8.3.5.5.8). This transform requires that the NVMe entity knows \(\mathsf{NQNAVE}\)  

<|ref|>text<|/ref|><|det|>[[114, 382, 883, 442]]<|/det|>
Derivation of \(\mathsf{PSK}_{\mathsf{ea}}\) from a DH- HMAC- CHAP secret is not possible if the DH- HMAC- CHAP secret representation does not specify a hash function. In this case \(\mathsf{PSK}_{\mathsf{ea}}\) shall be provisioned on the NVMe entity through a configured PSK, as specified for NVMe/TCP, and that configured PSK should be different than the DH- HMAC- CHAP secret for that entity.  

<|ref|>text<|/ref|><|det|>[[114, 448, 883, 478]]<|/det|>
Derivation of \(\mathsf{PSK}_{\mathsf{ea}}\) from a configured PSK shall use the HKDF- Extract and HKDF- Expand- Label operations in the following order:  

<|ref|>text<|/ref|><|det|>[[128, 484, 846, 515]]<|/det|>
1. PRK = HKDF-Extract(0, Configured PSK); and 
2. \(\mathsf{PSK}_{\mathsf{ea}} = \mathsf{HKDEF - Expand - Label}(\mathsf{PRK},\) "A-V-Entity" \(\parallel\) \(\mathsf{NQNAVE}\) \(\mathsf{NQN}_{\mathsf{e}}\) Length(Configured PSK)),  

<|ref|>text<|/ref|><|det|>[[114, 520, 883, 581]]<|/det|>
where \(\mathsf{NQNAVE}\) is the NQN of the AVE and \(\mathsf{NQN}_{\mathsf{e}}\) is the NQN of the NVMe entity. The hash function used with HKDF shall be the one specified in the PSK interchange format (refer to the NVMe over TCP Transport Specification). If no hash function is specified in the PSK interchange format, then the configured PSK shall be used as \(\mathsf{PSK}_{\mathsf{ea}}\) . This transform requires that the NVMe entity knows \(\mathsf{NQNAVE}\)  

<|ref|>text<|/ref|><|det|>[[114, 587, 883, 720]]<|/det|>
The TLS connection with the AVE shall be performed as specified in the TLS PSK and PSK Identity Derivation section of the NVMe over TCP Transport Specification, with \(\mathsf{PSK}_{\mathsf{ea}}\) used as the Retained PSK, \(\mathsf{NQN}_{\mathsf{e}}\) used as the host NQN, and \(\mathsf{NQNAVE}\) used as the controller NQN. Mandatory and recommended cipher suites for this TLS connection are specified in the Mandatory and Recommended Cipher Suites section of the NVMe over TCP Transport Specification. This TLS connection may be used for multiple authentication verifications. An NVMe entity may terminate this TLS connection and re- establish it as required. An AVE may terminate this TLS connection after some period of inactivity (e.g., 10 minutes). An NVMe entity may avoid termination of this TLS connection by using the TLS heartbeat extension (refer to RFC 6520).
