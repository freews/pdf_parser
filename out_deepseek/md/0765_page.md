#### 8.3.5.6.4 AVE Discovery  

<|ref|>text<|/ref|><|det|>[[114, 508, 883, 568]]<|/det|>
The AVE transport addresses may be configured on an NVMe entity or may be discovered by interacting with a Discovery Controller (e.g., a CDC). An NVMe entity should randomly select any of the discovered AVE transport addresses to connect to the AVE. The AVE Discovery log page is defined to facilitate this discovery (refer to section 5.2.12.3.5).
