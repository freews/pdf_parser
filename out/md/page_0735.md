NVM Express® Base Specification, Revision 2.3

**Figure 777: Example reference for retrieving Underlying Namespaces and Underlying Ports**

<!-- Figure 777, coordinate:(150,110,870,530) -->

The diagram illustrates the relationship between underlying namespaces, underlying ports, and exported NVM resources in an NVM Express system.

- **Underlying Namespace List** (yellow box at top) connects to two **Underlying Namespace** boxes via connection point **1**.
- These namespaces are associated with **NVM Storage** (a box containing three yellow "NVMe" and one "NVMe SSD" elements).
- The **Underlying Namespace** (left) connects via a dashed line to the **Exported NVM resource collection** (large box labeled with **3**).
- Inside the **Exported NVM resource collection**:
  - **Exported NVM Subsystem (SUBNQN)** contains:
    - **Exported Namespace** (labeled **4**)
    - **Exported Port ID** (labeled **5**)
    - **Allowed Host List** (labeled **6**)
- The **Exported Port ID** (5) connects via a dashed line to the **Underlying Ports List** (yellow box at bottom).
- The **Underlying Ports List** connects to three **Underlying Port** boxes:
  - **Underlying Port (Ethernet)**
  - **Underlying Port (Fibre channel)**
  - **Underlying Port (Infiniband)**
- These ports are linked together and connected to the **Underlying Ports List** via connection point **2**.

The diagram shows how exported namespaces and ports are derived from underlying physical resources and how they are mapped in the NVM subsystem.
